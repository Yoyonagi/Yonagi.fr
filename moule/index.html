The project

This project was carried out over my last two years at university. It is a plastic injection mould designed to produce a tensile test specimen for teaching purposes.

The 3D model shows the whole mould, with its fixed and moving halves. It was modelled in SolidWorks, machined using Fusion 360, then converted for viewing on the web.

The project is part of a machining simulation and optimisation approach. Several videos show the manufacturing steps and the tests carried out.
